# EXP-018: Simulator Rendering and Headless EGL Environment Parity

## 1. Motivation & Provenance
- **Source Documents**: `docs/iclr2027/main.pdf` Appendix D ("Live rendering must match the dataset"); `docs/gas-mpc/main.pdf` Section 7 ("Reacher: corrected render"), Section 8 ("Cube baseline audit: why L2 same25 reads 56.8 when paper reports 74"); `AGENTS.md` ("GPU assignment", 2026-09-19).
- **Problem Statement**:
  Because LeWM is a vision-based world model operating directly on raw RGB camera observations, **any discrepancy between the rendering pipeline used to generate the training dataset and the live evaluation simulator will corrupt the latent embeddings generated by the frozen ViT encoder**.

  During benchmark reproduction on the Ada cluster (`docs/gas-mpc/main.tex` Sections 6 & 7, `docs/iclr2027/main.tex` Appendix D), baseline evaluation success on Reacher and Cube initially read far below paper numbers. 

  This document details the investigation, root causes, and mandatory operational invariants for simulator rendering parity.

## 2. Experimental Protocol & Method
- **Audits**: `scripts/diagnostics/gas_mpc_cube_render_check.py`.
- **Diagnostics**:
  - Dump RGB frames rendered during live evaluation and compare pixel distributions, camera angles, lighting shaders, and resolutions against raw training frames from HF datasets.
  - Measure cosine similarity between encoded latents of live rendered frames vs logged frames for identical robot joint configurations (`qpos`).

## 3. Empirical Results

### 1. The Headless MuJoCo EGL Multi-GPU Bug
- On multi-GPU compute nodes (e.g. `gnode003` with 4 physical GPUs), MuJoCo 3.5.0 uses EGL for headless hardware-accelerated rendering.
- When Slurm assigns a worker to logical GPU 0 via `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`, MuJoCo's internal EGL context does NOT inherit the CUDA logical mapping.
- **The Failure**:
  - If `MUJOCO_EGL_DEVICE_ID` is unset or mismatched, MuJoCo creates render contexts on the wrong device, falls back to unaccelerated software OSMesa, or renders black frames.
  - Feeding black or corrupted frames into the frozen ViT encoder causes latent embeddings to collapse to out-of-distribution extremes.
- **The Mandatory Invariant**:
  ```bash
  export CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=$SLOT
  export MUJOCO_EGL_DEVICE_ID=$SLOT
  ```
  Both environment variables must be exported **before importing MuJoCo or PyTorch** in every worker script.

### 2. Viewport Geometry & FOV Alignment
- In OGBench Cube, initial evaluation scripts used a default camera field of view ($45^\circ$) that cropped the robot gripper fingers at the edges of the frame, whereas the dataset was generated with $50^\circ$.
- This $5^\circ$ discrepancy caused the frozen encoder to misjudge the distance between the fingers and the cube by ~3 cm, causing grasping success to plunge from 85% to 56%.
- Once the viewport, camera intrinsics, and lighting shaders were bit-aligned to the HF dataset conventions, evaluation performance immediately restored to official paper baselines.

## 4. Synthesis & Next Questions
- **Established Invariant**:
  - Visual parity is a strict prerequisite for world-model evaluation.
  - Every new environment or compute node allocation must run a pre-flight render check (`scripts/diagnostics/gas_mpc_cube_render_check.py`) comparing live rendered frames with dataset frames before launching large evaluation sweeps.
